# Startup Studio

Welcome! This is your Startup Studio workspace. You will go from a blank canvas to a live, public-facing website — step by step.

---

## Where to Start

Open the **Guide** panel in Codio and follow the lessons in order. They will walk you through every stage.

---

## Folder Structure

```
SPECS/              ← Your project constitution (read this first)
  MISSION.md        ← Your startup's purpose, audience, and rules
  TECH.md           ← The tech stack and engineering rules for this project
  ROADMAP.md        ← The 6-stage journey from idea to live site

build-lab/          ← Your working folder — all website files go here
  MISSION.md        ← Your Founder Notebook (fill this in as you go)
  index.html        ← Your landing page
  style.css         ← Your styles and design tokens
  script.js         ← Your interactions and animations
```

---

## The Golden Rule

> **Think → Ask → Evaluate → Decide → Build**

Never just ask OpenCode for something and accept the result. You are the founder. You make the decisions.

---

## Quick Reference

- **Constitution:** `SPECS/` — the rules and roadmap for this project
- **Your notebook:** `build-lab/MISSION.md` — fill this in at every stage
- **Your website:** `build-lab/index.html` — this is what visitors will see
